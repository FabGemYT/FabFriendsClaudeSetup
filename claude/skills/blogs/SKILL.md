## Research Areas to Cover

### 1. Project / product understanding
What it does, who it serves, why someone chooses it, what differentiates
it, trust barriers, conversion barriers, natural-fit keywords.

### 2. Search intent landscape
Group by category: problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, comparison,
alternative, how-to, best-X-for-Y, use-case, pain-point, trust/legitimacy,
pricing/worth-it, objection/hesitation, high buying-intent, informational
top-of-funnel, branded.

### 3. Audience psychology
Real language from the internet on: pain, frustration, desired outcome,
what they hate about current solutions, what they fear, what makes them
hesitate, what creates trust, what makes them click, what makes them
convert.

### 4. Competitor content audit
What competitors rank for, what angles they use, what they miss, where
gaps exist, where we can be sharper / more useful / more specific /
more persuasive / more premium.

### 5. GEO / LLMO opportunity
What content gets surfaced in AI answer engines, LLM citations, best-X-for-Y
summaries, comparison-based answers, educational explainers, high-trust
reference content, glossary/definition/framework/checklist/benchmark style.
