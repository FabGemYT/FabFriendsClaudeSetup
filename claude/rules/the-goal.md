# The goal

The owner pasted this on 36 different days across five projects before it became a standing
rule (2026-11-10). It applies to every customer-facing word without being pasted again:

> the goal is SEO GEO LLMO AEO MAX as well as gsc indexnow indexing ranking max, while also
> making sure everything is hormozi playbook in the project repo or if it's not in the project
> repo it's made from the hormozi books in the download folder, 5th to 7th grade where possible
> not everywhere, /unslop skill used sitewide, fomo max, dopamine max, heart winning max,
> psychologically max on customers minds and with max out research brainstorm on copy
> positioning layout organizing reasoning and everything, and nothing is brainlessly or ai
> sloply written, and all the website built professionally like actual proper websites like the
> list i gave, and better than them, this is the goal and hopefully you follow it sitewide for
> every single word

Quote it at the top of the plan for any customer-facing work (a site makeover did), then
judge every change by it. The project's own rules (its CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, PRD or plan) win
wherever they are stricter or more specific.

## Six checks for every change (a second online store' goal list, 15 Oct 2026)

1. **Search:** SEO, GEO, LLMO and AEO; Search Console and indexability; IndexNow and fresh
   discovery, done correctly.
2. **Buyers:** conversion, trust and clarity; Hormozi's playbook; strong psychology; a clear
   scent from the search or ad to the answer; as little uncertainty as possible.
3. **Words:** grade 5 to 7 where it fits; /unslop on all of it; dense with information; easy.
4. **Proof:** first-party evidence (our own numbers, photos, certificates, reviews). Never invented.
5. **Build:** excellent human UI and UX, and excellent machine readability.
6. **Method:** maximum research, brainstorming and reasoning, nothing done on autopilot. Read the
   history first, verify every claim, proven pipelines before new ones, the smallest change that
   works, tested on real data, nothing paid left running.

## Before any words: research, then positioning

- **Research the buyer, not just the competition:** their own words (reviews, Reddit, support
  chats, comments), the best sites in the niche (The owner's reference list when there is one),
  what competitors claim. Several outside sources, never one competitor: copying one store
  copies its mistakes too (a second online store).
- **One line of positioning before any copy** (a site makeover opened with it).
- **Write down what is frozen** (pages, numbers and claims the owner treats as the truth)
  before changing anything around them.
- **Options before build** on positioning and layout (CLAUDE.md Law 6).

## Hormozi: the project's playbook first, then the books

1. **Use the project's own playbook when it has one:** a Hormozi playbook in the repo (docs/),
   in the project's plan folder, or in its Claude memory (`reference/HORMOZI_PLAYBOOK.md`).
2. **None? Build one for this project from the three books and save it with the project**
   (outside git when it quotes the books), so it is built once:
   - The books: `~/Downloads/Alex Hormozi Books/` holds `100M-Offers.pdf` (138 pages),
     `100M-Leads.pdf` (385) and `100M-Money.pdf` (188). On another machine, look for a folder
     named `Alex Hormozi Books` in Downloads.
   - Read only the chapters this project needs (map below), as text, which is far cheaper than
     page images: `pdftotext -f <first> -l <last> -layout "<book>.pdf" -`.
   - **Never commit, upload or paste the PDFs, or long passages from them, anywhere.** Notes
     in your own words only.
   - No books on this machine: use the core below and tell the owner once that the books would
     make it sharper.
3. **The core every playbook starts from** (the an online store and a second online store playbooks, both
   built from the books):
   - **Value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort and sacrifice).**
     Most sellers only inflate the top. Shrink the bottom: sell the outcome, prove it, cut the
     wait, cut the effort (a site makeover).
   - **Grand Slam Offer:** list every problem the buyer has, turn each into a solution, pick how
     to deliver it, then trim and stack into one named bundle that cannot be compared on price.
   - **Name the offer's parts. Value before price. Add a bonus, never cut the price**
     (a second online store).
   - **Only real scarcity and urgency:** a real cut-off, a real capacity, an announced price
     rise (a second online store' live clock to the 3 pm dispatch cut-off).
   - **A guarantee you can police:** conditional, tied to proof you can check.
   - **Money model order:** attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity. The main offer first, the
     cheaper path only after a no (a live web game). A downsell changes how they pay or what they
     get, never the same thing for less.
   - **Leads:** the Core Four (warm outreach, free content, cold outreach, paid ads), then lead
     getters (referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates). Lifetime gross profit to acquisition
     cost of at least 3 to 1; aim to earn back acquisition cost within 30 days.
4. **Chapter map** (PDF page numbers, checked 2026-11-10):
   - **Offers:** Grand Slam Offers 25; the commodity problem 34; finding a starving crowd 41;
     charge what it's worth 49; the value equation 61; the thought process 74; problems and

   - **Leads:** engaging leads, offers and lead magnets 43; warm outreach 76; free content 108

## Words, on every line

From a second online store' copy rulebook and a site makeover:

- **Grade 5 to 7 where it fits:** heroes, headlines, buttons, offers, emails, captions.
  Science, spec tables, legal and technical pages stay exact. Measure it, do not guess:
  `py -3 ~/.claude/bin/reading-grade.py <url or file>` (the an online store check, same formula).
- **Facts first** (price, size, stock, delivery time): Google and AI answers lift the first
  clear fact, and statistics with citations raised visibility in AI answers by up to 40%
  (Aggarwal and others, "GEO", KDD 2024).
- **The first sentence of a page defines its subject:** it is the sentence quoted for "what is X".
- **A number beats an adjective, and a sharp number beats a round one** (real numbers only).
- **Written as an established brand**, never as a launch.
- **Every number comes from one data file**, so two pages can never disagree.
- **No em dashes, no AI phrases, no placeholders, no fake "was" prices.**
- **/unslop is the last pass on every page's copy**; impeccable for UI labels, errors and
  empty states.
- **Never invent a review, a number, a rating or a result.**

## FOMO, dopamine, heart: as strong as possible, and always true

- **Every section names the job it does in the reader's mind, and why** (a live web game's creator
  page): a head start on a progress bar (goal gradient: Kivetz 2006; Nunes and Drèze 2006), a
  big dream outcome with many small wins, "how it works" in three plain steps for perceived
  likelihood, short firm rules for trust.
- **FOMO from real things only:** a real deadline, real stock, real popularity. Counters and
  "people viewing" only from live data. Fake scarcity breaks trust, and India's consumer
  regulator banned false urgency, basket sneaking, confirm shaming and drip pricing in its
  2023 dark-pattern guidelines.
- **Dopamine from small wins:** instant feedback, progress, a part-filled bar, a stamp earned,
  one offer at the moment of yes (a second online store' "make it 5" meter).
- **Heart from real people:** the customer's own words, real faces, a real person to talk to
  (a site makeover's "a real person on WhatsApp, day or night").

## Getting found: SEO, GEO, LLMO, AEO and indexing

From the a second online store launch gate and the an online store indexing routine:

- **Every page:** its own title and description, one H1, canonical, real internal links, fast
  (Core Web Vitals), and structured data: Organization, Product with price and availability
  (never a rating you cannot show), FAQ, Breadcrumb, Article. Skills: seo-technical,
  seo-schema, seo-page, seo-geo, ai-seo, seo-aeo-best-practices, core-web-vitals, site-architecture.
- **AI answers:** a short answer block near the top of each key page, facts with sources, and
  llms.txt (plus llms-full.txt). llms.txt does nothing for Google, but it costs nothing.
- **Sitemaps split by page type;** a page's date moves only when its own content changes.
- **Launch gate:** nothing goes to Search Console, Bing or IndexNow until a launch checklist has
  passed, because the first version Google sees has to be right.
- **After launch:**
  - Search Console: the domain property verified by DNS, every sitemap submitted;
    `py -3 ~/.claude/bin/gsc.py` for coverage, inspection and traffic.
  - Bing Webmaster Tools: verified (a CNAME on the DNS host), sitemaps submitted.
  - IndexNow, for changed pages only, after each production deploy or daily:
    `node ~/.claude/bin/indexnow.mjs <host> --changed-days 3` (a key file in `public/`).
  - About 10 "Request indexing" a day for new and changed key pages, through Search Console
    in the Claude browser (Google's daily allowance). Never the Indexing API (job and
    livestream pages only) and never paid "instant indexing".
- **Measure before claiming a ranking or an indexing result:** `gsc.py totals | top | pages | inspect`.
